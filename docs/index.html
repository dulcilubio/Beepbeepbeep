1. Introduction

Beep Beep Beep ("the app", "we", "us") is an iOS application designed as a precision start clock for sailing races and similar timed events. It synchronises to GPS time and plays audio signals at configurable intervals.

This Privacy Policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and what rights you have.

By using Beep Beep Beep, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Data We Access

2.1 Location / GPS (CoreLocation)

Beep Beep Beep requests When In Use location access solely to obtain the timestamp of the GPS fix. The app reads the time embedded in the satellite signal (CLLocation.timestamp) to synchronise its internal clock with GPS time. No geographic coordinates, routes, or movement data are recorded, stored, or transmitted by the app.

Data accessedPurposeStored?Transmitted?
GPS timestampClock synchronisationNoNo
GPS coordinatesNot usedNoNo

You can deny location permission and the app will fall back to NTP or device clock.

2.2 Network Time Protocol (NTP)

When GPS is unavailable or permission is not granted, the app queries public NTP servers to synchronise its clock. NTP is a standard internet time protocol; no personal data is included in NTP packets. As with any internet request, NTP servers receive your device's IP address.

2.3 Local Settings (AppStorage)

User preferences (start interval, beep pitch, display font, etc.) are stored locally in iOS UserDefaults. This data never leaves your device.

2.4 Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any account information
  • We do not record or store your GPS location
  • We do not collect advertising identifiers (IDFA) or perform device fingerprinting
  • We do not use analytics SDKs (e.g. Firebase, Amplitude, Mixpanel)
  • We do not use third-party crash reporting services
  • We do not operate any backend servers
